This policy explains what information the Android app Koeto Memo (Japanese name: コエトメモ; "the app"; application ID: com.totokotolab.koetomemo) stores, where it is stored, and what it is used for.

1. Summary

  • Your memos, recordings and settings are stored on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • The app has no developer server. It does not send your memos, recordings or settings to the developer.
  • There is no account or sign-in, and no ads.
  • When the app turns your voice into text, it passes the recording's audio to your device's speech-recognition service. Depending on the service, it may use the network (section 5).
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play (section 6).

3. Information the developer receives

  • The app does not send your memos, recordings or settings to the developer (the app has no developer server).
  • The app itself does not include any advertising, usage-analytics or crash-reporting mechanism. However, components provided by Google, such as the Google Play Billing Library, may handle operational information (such as whether calls succeeded and the connection state) for processing purchases and improving the library, in line with Google's terms. On inspecting the Billing Library parts included in the app, we found that they contain a feature that builds diagnostic records (such as the app's package name and version, the Android version and the device model) and sends them through Google's transport mechanism. However, what is actually sent, and how long Google keeps it and what it uses it for, could not be confirmed from published materials as of this policy's preparation. Please see Google's explanations.
  • For purchases, through Google Play the developer can see order information in the Google Play Console. What is visible is governed by Google's terms. The content of your memos and recordings never reaches the developer.
  • If you e-mail us, we use your e-mail address and message only to reply and respond. You do not need to attach memos or recordings.

4. Activity records (logs)

To investigate problems, the app may keep activity records on your device (such as recording length, counts and error numbers). It does not record the text of your memos or the content of your recordings. The app does not send these records to the developer automatically.

5. Voice-to-text and external services

  • When you save a voice memo, the app automatically passes the recording's audio to your device's speech-recognition service to produce text (Android 13 or later). It passes the audio in the same way when you press the button to transcribe again.
  • Which speech-recognition service is used depends on your device settings. The app does not choose the service or control how it works.
  • The app uses the device's standard speech-recognition service, and does not use an on-device-only recognizer or an offline-only setting. According to Android's official documentation, this kind of speech recognition is likely to stream audio to remote servers. On your device, therefore, the audio may be sent over the network to the service provider's servers and processed there.
  • Whether the audio actually leaves your device, where it is processed and whether it is stored depend on the service and its settings, and the app cannot verify this. The provider's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing, and the app cannot guarantee how it is handled. If this concerns you, please check the explanation from the provider of your device's speech-recognition service.
  • The app uses the audio of a recording for only two things: (1) playing the recording back, and (2) passing it to the speech-recognition service in order to turn it into text. It is not used for advertising or any other purpose. The app does not provide or sell your memos, recordings or settings to advertisers or other third parties (except passing audio to the speech-recognition service as described here, and purchase processing by Google Play).
  • If you prefer not to use this feature, you can write memos by typing (no microphone permission is needed).
  • On devices before Android 13, voice memos are not turned into text (recording and playback still work).

6. Purchases (Google Play) and the free trial

  • The app is free to download, and all features are available for 14 days from the first launch.
  • After the trial ends, a one-time purchase is required to create new memos or edit memos (fixing the text of a voice memo and re-running transcription count as editing). The price is shown in Google Play. There are no ads, subscriptions or automatic charges.
  • After the trial ends without a purchase, you can still view saved memos, play recordings, delete memos one by one, reorder them, and use widgets to display memos and choose which memo to show.
  • Purchase, payment and purchase records are handled by Google Play. Payment details such as card numbers are not passed to the app.
  • To check whether you have purchased, the app queries Google Play (the app uses the Google Play Billing Library provided by Google). The app receives the purchase state from Google Play, and sends the information used to confirm the purchase (the purchase token) only back to Google Play for the confirmation step; it does not store it on your device or send it to the developer. On your device it stores only a "purchased or not" record. Google's terms apply to the information Google handles when confirming a purchase and when the Billing Library communicates with Google Play.
  • With the same Google account, you can restore your purchase after reinstalling or on another device using "Restore purchase". Memos and recordings are not restored (section 8).

7. Network use

The app does not communicate with any developer server. Network communication may occur only:

  1. to get purchase information and complete a purchase (with Google Play, through the Google Play Billing Library), and
  2. when your device's speech-recognition service turns voice into text (the service may use the network; section 5).

The app may also check whether your device can reach the network, in order to explain whether a transcription failure was caused by connectivity (it only checks the state and does not communicate).

This does not mean the app never uses the network.

8. Backup, changing phones and reinstalling

  • The app is configured so that memos, recordings and settings are excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • As a result, when you change phones or reinstall, memos and recordings are not carried over. The app currently has no feature to move memos to another device. Please note down anything you need beforehand.
  • We have not verified how manufacturer-specific migration tools treat this setting. Behavior may differ by manufacturer and model.
  • Your purchase can be restored with the same Google account using "Restore purchase".

9. Retention and deletion

  • Memos stay on your device until you delete them.
  • To delete a memo, press "Remove" (はがす) on that memo and confirm. It is deleted immediately, together with its recording, and this cannot be undone.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in your device settings, removes everything the app stored (memos, recordings, settings and trial records). Your purchase entitlement remains with Google Play.
  • The developer does not hold your memos or recordings, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete.

10. Permissions

  • Microphone: used to record voice memos. The app explains why when it asks. You can still write memos by typing without it. You can change the permission in your device settings at any time.
  • Internet / network state, and billing: used by the Google Play Billing Library (and the Google-provided components it includes) to get purchase information and complete purchases. The app itself uses "network state" only to check whether the device can reach the network (section 7).

The app does not use location, contacts, photos or notification permissions.

11. Widgets and what appears on your screen

The app's widgets show memo text on your home screen. Please be careful where other people can see your screen.

12. Personal information

The app does not ask you to enter personal information such as your name, e-mail address or phone number.

13. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised.
